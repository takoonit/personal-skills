PASS only if all of these hold:
- Selects one primary style and explains why from the brief, saying the choice was its own.
- The written design rules are concrete (named tokens, type, spacing, focal element), with three to five binding rules for the style.
- Labels tokens and components as proposals, without claiming they are implemented or verified in a browser.

FAIL if any of these happen:
- Offers several styles without choosing one.
- Claims the design was rendered or tested.
