# Work Item Investigate

Run the `investigate` phase through the [phase chain](../work-item-delivery/references/chain.md). Its card is `docs/phases/investigate.md` under the runtime, and the card owns this phase's inputs, actions and stop conditions.

On Claude Code, send investigation briefs to `work-item-investigator` and the challenge brief to `work-item-challenger` as [Claude Code](../work-item-delivery/references/claude-code.md) describes.
