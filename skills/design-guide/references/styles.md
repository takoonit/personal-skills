# Choose a style, then load its guide

Read this selection map and `foundations.md`, then only the chosen style's detailed guide. Read one alternative if comparison is needed. Do not load all seven guides into an ordinary task or copy the catalogue into DESIGN.md.

These are design judgements, not measured demographic preferences or a ranking of 2026 popularity. Audience fit depends on situation, content and cultural context. Do not infer taste from age or income alone.

## Selection map

| Style and detailed guide | Useful audience situation / use case | Distinctive decision | Main cost or trap |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Neoskeuomorphism](style-neoskeuomorphism.md) | People exploring a sensory product or manipulating a tactile tool | A coherent material and lighting system explains interaction | Weak boundaries, inconsistent material and rendering cost |
| [Kinetic typography](style-kinetic-typography.md) | Visitors to a campaign, cultural event or message-led launch | The message is the moving focal element | Reading delays, motion sensitivity and localisation |
| [Intentional minimalism](style-intentional-minimalism.md) | Focused services, considered purchases, recurring tasks | A small number of recognisable details carry the identity | Generic templates or useful information removed for emptiness |
| [Editorial design](style-editorial.md) | Readers, researchers and visitors comparing substantial stories or work | Type, images and captions organise the reading sequence | Content demands, fragile layouts and mobile reading order |
| [Story-driven animation](style-story-animation.md) | People learning an unfamiliar mechanism, process or product | A meaningful change makes the explanation easier to understand | Forced playback, false progress and expensive assets |
| [Human-made design](style-human-made.md) | Communities and customers interested in people, place or provenance | Specific authored assets give the product a recognisable voice | Missing original assets or fabricated authenticity |
| [Expressive design](style-expressive.md) | Cultural audiences, entertainment communities and identity-led brands | A consistent bold visual grammar reflects the brand | Competing focal points, narrower appeal and unusable controls |

Keep exactly one primary style. The categories overlap; they are working directions, not an exhaustive taxonomy. Select according to the governing composition, not a lone effect. Glass alone does not make a system neoskeuomorphic; large text does not make it kinetic; grain does not establish human provenance.

## Calibrate expression by surface

| Surface | Where to concentrate expression | What must remain dependable |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign / landing page | Opening composition, meaningful star, selected narrative moments | Offer comprehension, navigation, main action and page loading |
| Product workspace / repeated task | Main task area, useful state feedback, restrained recurring details | Scan speed, input behaviour, density and recovery |
| Reading / comparison | Titles, image relationships, pacing and section markers | Reading order, captions, comparison details and findability |
| Checkout / settings / critical action | Existing type, shape, colour and small details | Labels, prices, decisions, validation and confirmation |

This changes intensity, not the primary style. A task surface must not inherit a landing-page spectacle simply to look consistent.

## Convert the guide into a project contract

1. Identify the project's material, content, message or cultural idea that makes this style specific.
2. Resolve all six techniques using the selected guide and actual content/assets.
3. Select three to five binding style rules. For each name its scope, token/component or proposed destination, allowed exception and review evidence. Combine overlapping rules; do not impose every suggestion in the guide.
4. Resolve concrete choices: type roles, colour relationships, spacing/density, shape/depth grammar, image treatment and motion behaviour. Reuse incumbent values; label proposed values.
5. Carry the highest-risk style rules into the concise guardrails. A builder must not be able to follow every bullet while accidentally producing a different style.

Profile defaults are recommendations until selected for the project. User constraints, accessibility needs and coherent incumbent rules outrank them. Explain a departure only when it affects the chosen character or verification; avoid an exception register for trivial details.

## Reference sites

These examples were identified during the 2026-10-01 research. They illustrate selected traits, not all six techniques, measured popularity or proof of commercial effectiveness. Check the relevant page before describing its current appearance or motion. Do not copy a site's assets or brand identity.

| Style | Reference sites | What to study |
|---|---|---|
| Neoskeuomorphism | [Jords+Co](https://www.jords.co.uk/), [Poolsuite](https://poolsuite.net/) | Tactile detail; Poolsuite is an adjacent classic desktop metaphor, not a pure modern example |
| Kinetic typography | [Wodwo](https://wodwo.co/how-it-works/), [G-uld](https://www.g-uld.dk/) | Typographic composition and, when verified live, motion rhythm |
| Intentional minimalism | [Aesop](https://www.aesop.com/), [COS](https://www.cos.com/en-us) | Product focus, restrained identity and content hierarchy |
| Editorial design | [The Gentlewoman](https://thegentlewoman.co.uk/), [It's Nice That](https://www.itsnicethat.com/) | Image/type relationships, captions, indexes and reading rhythm |
| Story-driven animation | [Apple AirPods Pro](https://www.apple.com/airpods-pro/), [Species in Pieces](https://species-in-pieces.com/) | Product explanation and staged transformation; the older Species reference has unverified live animation availability |
| Human-made design | [Patagonia Stories](https://www.patagonia.com/stories/), [Oatly](https://www.oatly.com/) | Documentary specificity and authored illustration/lettering |
| Expressive design | [Gumroad](https://gumroad.com/), [MSCHF](https://mschf.com/) | Coherent bold graphics and identity; boldness alone does not imply premium quality |

Use [Fonts In Use](https://fontsinuse.com/) for pairing research when useful. Verify availability, licensing and required scripts before selecting any font.
