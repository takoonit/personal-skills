# Shark Tank

Give a candid commercial judgment through three investor perspectives. Ground the verdict in the business's maturity and the evidence available.

## Investor perspectives

- **Hook:** Does a specific customer care enough to act? Examine current behavior, substitutes, willingness to pay, distribution, and repeat use.
- **Moat:** Why can this business win? Examine differentiation, founder insight, access, switching costs, and competitive response. An early idea needs a plausible wedge; an established business needs evidence of a durable advantage.
- **Ledger:** Is the next investment justified? Examine the payer, pricing, costs, operating burden, accessible market, and opportunity cost.

Use these as reasoning lenses. Do not invent disagreement or require separate agents.

## Approach

1. Reuse facts already supplied. Ask only founder-specific questions that could change the decision; research public facts when current external evidence matters. Read [interview and research guidance](references/business-evidence.md) when the pitch is incomplete or market research is needed.
2. Identify maturity: **Idea**, **Validation**, **Early traction**, or **Business**. At idea stage, prioritize the problem and demand. With traction, examine retention and acquisition. Require repeatable economics before recommending scale.
3. Distinguish observed behavior, supported external evidence, unvalidated claims, and unknowns. Cite consequential external claims and expose conflicting evidence.
4. Find the binding constraint across the three perspectives. Choose the cheapest decisive next test and, when justified, a condition for stopping.

Never fabricate revenue, market size, conversion, CAC, LTV, retention, or margins. Compliments and hypothetical intent are weaker than behavior and payment. A large market alone does not establish an accessible opportunity. Research and recommendations do not authorize spending, outreach, or experiments on real customers.

## Finish

Give each relevant perspective a short assessment, then one overall verdict: **Pass**, **Prove**, **Validate**, **Invest**, or **Scale**. Explain the binding constraint and uncertainty without averaging votes or inventing precise confidence scores.

State the next bounded bet, evidence that would change the verdict, and a walk-away condition where defensible. If the pitch cannot support a judgment, say **Too early to call** and identify the missing evidence.

Stop when the commercial question is answered. Suggest `strategic-gate` only for unresolved prioritization or `shape-system-work` for product direction that still needs a decision.
